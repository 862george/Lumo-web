About SynthPage

A small studio that keeps things clean.

SynthPage is a small web design studio on the south coast of England. We build clean, fast, mobile-friendly websites for local businesses such as tradespeople, salons, cafés, groomers, studios and shops, and we look after the hosting so you don’t have to.

The name comes from how a synthesiser works: it takes a raw signal and shapes it into something clean and clear. That’s what we do with your business’s presence online.

We start with a free mock-up, so you can see your website before you decide anything. Once you approve it, we publish it and keep it running for a simple monthly fee.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a website cost?

A Basic website has a one-off setup fee of £150, then £15 a month for hosting, small updates and support. An Advanced website has a one-off setup fee of £230 (usually £250), then £25 a month. We confirm the exact price in writing before we begin. Prices don’t include VAT because we aren’t VAT-registered.

What do I get for the monthly fee?

Hosting, small updates and support, so your website stays online and up to date. You can cancel any time by emailing contact@synthpage.co.uk, and we’ll give you a copy of your site’s files if you ask.

Will I see the website before I commit?

Yes. We build a mock-up first, with your business name and colours, and nothing is published until you approve it. The setup fee includes 2 rounds of changes to the mock-up.

What’s the difference between Basic and Advanced?

A Basic website is clean, fast and mobile-friendly. An Advanced website includes everything in Basic plus smooth animations, custom email addresses for your business, and priority support, so your requests go to the front of the queue.

What are custom emails?

We set up email addresses on your business’s own domain, such as hello@yourbusiness.co.uk, so you look more professional than with a free Gmail or Outlook address. Custom emails are included in the Advanced plan.

What do you need from me?

A few details about your business, plus any text, photos and your logo that you’d like us to use. If you don’t have these yet, tell us and we’ll talk through the options.

Who owns my content?

You do. You keep ownership of your own text, images and logos. We may show screenshots of the finished website in our portfolio unless you tell us not to.

Do you build online shops or booking systems?

SynthPage focuses on clean, simple business websites. If you need something more complex, email us and we’ll tell you honestly whether we’re the right fit.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?

We’re on the south coast of England and work with local businesses around Portsmouth and Chichester, such as tradespeople, salons, cafés, groomers, studios and shops. Because websites are delivered online, we can also work with businesses elsewhere in the UK.
