About SynthPage

Last updated: 9 November 2026

SynthPage is a small web design studio on the south coast of England. We design, build and host clean, fast, mobile-friendly websites for local businesses. You can reach us at contact@synthpage.co.uk.

What we do

We build simple, good-looking websites that make a business easy to find and easy to trust. Every website is designed around the business’s name, colours and logo, works well on phones, and is hosted and looked after by us for a small monthly fee. We start with a free mock-up, and nothing is published until the customer approves it.

Who we work with

Local businesses and organisations such as tradespeople (plumbers, electricians), salons, cafés and takeaways, dog groomers, recording studios, clubs and shops. We are based around Portsmouth and Chichester and serve the wider South Coast. Websites are delivered online, so we can also work with businesses elsewhere in the UK.

Our two website packages

  • Basic website: £150 one-off setup fee, then £15 a month for hosting, small updates and support. A clean, fast, mobile-friendly website designed around your business and colours.
  • Advanced website: £230 one-off setup fee (usually £250), then £25 a month. Everything in Basic, plus animations, custom email addresses for your business, and priority support.

Prices are in pounds sterling and don’t include VAT because we aren’t VAT-registered. We confirm the exact price in writing before we begin work, and customers can cancel the monthly service at any time by emailing us.

How it works

  1. We chat. You tell us about your business and what you need.
  2. We build it. You see a mock-up first, with your name and colours. The setup fee includes 2 rounds of changes.
  3. You go live. You approve it and we publish it, with hosting looked after.

Why “SynthPage”?

A synthesiser takes a raw signal and shapes it into something clean and clear. A page is where a customer first meets your business online. SynthPage shapes your business into a clean, clear page. Our logo is a square wave, one of the basic shapes a synthesiser makes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?
A Basic website has a one-off setup fee of £150, then £15 a month for hosting, small updates and support. An Advanced website has a one-off setup fee of £230 (usually £250), then £25 a month. We confirm the exact price in writing before we begin. Prices don’t include VAT because we aren’t VAT-registered.

What do I get for the monthly fee?
Hosting, small updates and support, so your website stays online and up to date. You can cancel any time by emailing contact@synthpage.co.uk, and we’ll give you a copy of your site’s files if you ask.

Will I see the website before I commit?
Yes. We build a mock-up first, with your business name and colours, and nothing is published until you approve it. The setup fee includes 2 rounds of changes to the mock-up.

What’s the difference between Basic and Advanced?
A Basic website is clean, fast and mobile-friendly. An Advanced website includes everything in Basic plus smooth animations, custom email addresses for your business, and priority support, so your requests go to the front of the queue.

What are custom emails?
We set up email addresses on your business’s own domain, such as hello@yourbusiness.co.uk, so you look more professional than with a free Gmail or Outlook address. Custom emails are included in the Advanced plan.

What do you need from me?
A few details about your business, plus any text, photos and your logo that you’d like us to use. If you don’t have these yet, tell us and we’ll talk through the options.

Who owns my content?
You do. You keep ownership of your own text, images and logos. We may show screenshots of the finished website in our portfolio unless you tell us not to.

Do you build online shops or booking systems?
SynthPage focuses on clean, simple business websites. If you need something more complex, email us and we’ll tell you honestly whether we’re the right fit.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?
We’re on the south coast of England and work with local businesses around Portsmouth and Chichester, such as tradespeople, salons, cafés, groomers, studios and shops. Because websites are delivered online, we can also work with businesses elsewhere in the UK.
